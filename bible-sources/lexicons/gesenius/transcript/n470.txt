RUNNING HEADS: מדי־מה | CCCCLI | מדר־מה [?]
Gentile noun emphat. מָדָיָא כתיב מָדָאָה, a *Mede*, Dan. 6:1.

מַדַּי [?] (contr. from מָה־דַּי) *what is sufficient*, 2 Ch. 30:3, compare the note under מָה.

מִדַּי [?] see דַּי No. 2, *b*.

מִדְיָן m.—(1) *strife, contention* (from the root דִּין see Niphal), pl. מִדְיָנִים Prov. 18:18; 19:13, and elsewhere in קרי, where כתיב has מִדְוָנִים. See מָדוֹן.
(2) [*Midian*], pr. n. of an Arabian nation, descended from Abraham (Gen. 25:2), whose territory lay from the eastern shore of the Ælanitic gulf (where the Arabian geographers place the city مدين), as far as the land of Moab, and appears to have extended to Mount Sinai, Exod. 3:1; 18:5; Num. 31; Jud. 6—8. In some passages the Midianites seem nearly identified with the Ishmaelites, Gen. 37:25, compare 36; Jud. 7:12, compare 8:22, 24, from whom, however, in other places, they are distinguished, Gen. 25:2, 4, 12—18.—בִּכְרֵי מִדְיָן the dromedaries of Midian, Isa. 60:6; יוֹם מִדְיָן the day of Midian, Isa. 9:3, i. e. the victory gained over the Midianites (compare Jud. chap. 7, 8.)—The Gentile noun is מִדְיָנִי a *Midianite*, Num. 10:29; pl. ־ִים Gen. 37:28; f. ־ִית Num. 25:15.

מִדִּין ("measures"), [*Middin*], pr. n. of a town in the plain country of the tribe of Judah, Josh. 15:61.

מְדִינָה fem. (from the root דִּין) prop. *judgment, jurisdiction*; hence—
(1) *a province*, allotted to the jurisdiction of a prefect or viceroy, as was the case with the provinces and satrapies of Persia, Est. 1:1, 22; 3:12, 14; בְּנֵי הַמְּדִינָה Ezr. 2:1; Neh. 7:6 (the Israelites), inhabitants of provinces.
(2) *a country, a land*, Dan. 11:24; Lam. 1:1; Eze. 19:8; Ecc. 2:8 (compare Ezr. 4:13; 5:7.) See the following word—

מְדִינָה f. Ch.—(1) *a province*, Dan. 3:2, 3.
(2) *land, country*, Dan. 2:48, 49; 3:1, 12, 30; Ezr. 5:8. (Syr [Syriac] id. [This word means *city*, as it is corrected in Thes.]; but مدينة signifies *city*.)

מְדֹכָה *a mortar*, Num. 11:8, from the root דּוּךְ.

מַדְמֵן ("dunghill"), [*Madmen*], pr. n. of a town in the borders of Moab, Jer. 48:2.

מַדְמֵנָה f.—(1) i. q. דֹּמֶן *dunghill*, Isa. 25:10.

=== RIGHT COLUMN ===
(2) [*Madmenah*], pr. n. of a town in the tribe of Benjamin, not far from Jerusalem, Isa. 10:31.

מַדְמַנָּה ("dunghill"), [*Madmannah*], pr. n. of a town in the tribe of Judah, Josh. 15:31.

מָדָן (from the root דִּין).—(1) *strife, contention*, only in pl. מְדָנִים Prov. 6:14, 19; 10:12.
(2) [*Medan*], pr. n. of a son of Abraham and Keturah, brother of Midian, Gen. 25:2.

מְדָנִי Gentile noun, only in pl. מְדָנִים i. q. מִדְיָנִים *Midianites*, Gen. 37:36, compare verse 28.

מַדָּע & מַדַּע [?] (from the root יָדַע [" י compensated by Dagesh as in verbs פ"ן]), a word found in the later Hebrew.
(1) *knowledge*, 2 Ch. 1:10, 11, 12; Dan. 1:4, 17.
(2) *the mind, the soul*, Ecc. 10:20, "even in thy mind curse not the king." LXX. συνείδησις. (Ch. מַנְדַּע, Syr. [Syriac] id.)

מֹדַע see מוֹדָע.

מַדְקְרוֹת pl. f. (from the root דָּקַר) *piercings* (of the sword), Prov. 12:18.

מְדָר Ch. i. q. מָדוֹר (from the root דּוּר) *habitation*, Dan. 2:11.

מַדְרֵגָה fem. *a steep mountain*, which one has to ascend *by steps*, as though it were *a ladder* (Felsensteige, Felsentreppe, compare κλίμαξ e. g. κλίμαξ Τυρίων), from the root דָּרַג Cant. 2:14; Eze. 38:20.

מִדְרָךְ m. (from the root דָּרַךְ) *a place which is trodden*, Deu. 2:5.

מִדְרָשׁ m. (from the root דָּרַשׁ No. 5), *a commentary*, as often in the Rabbinic. 2 Ch. 24:27, מִדְרַשׁ סֵפֶר הַמְּלָכִים "the commentary of (or on) the book of the kings," i. e. an historical commentary containing supplements. 2 Ch. 13:22. Others suppose that מִדְרָשׁ like the Arab. مدرس means any book, but this is incorrect.

מְדָתָא with the art. הַמְּדָתָא pr. n. (Pers.) of the father of Haman, Est. 3:1; 8:5.
