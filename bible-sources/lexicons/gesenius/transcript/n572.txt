Derivative מַסָּה.
נָסַח fut. יִסַּח TO PLUCK OUT,—(a) any one from his house, Ps. 52:7; from the land, i. e. to drive into exile, Prov. 2:22.—(b) used of a house, i. e. to destroy, Prov. 15:25; men and houses, like plants, are said both to be planted and plucked up; compare נָטַע and נָתַשׁ.
NIPHAL *to be plucked up*, i. e. expelled (from a land), Deu. 28:63.
Derivative מַסָּח.
נְסַח Chald. i. q. Hebr. ITHPEAL pass. Ezr. 6:11.
נָסִיךְ m. (from the root נָסַךְ)—(1) *a libation, a drink offering*, Deu. 32:38.
(2) *a molten image*, i. q. מַסֵּכָה Dan. 11:8.
(3) *one anointed*, i. e. *a prince* consecrated by anointing, i. q. מָשִׁיחַ, but more poetic in its use, Jos. 13:21; Ps. 83:12; Eze. 32:30; Mic. 5:4.
I. נָסַךְ i. q. סוּךְ (compare No. 3) TO POUR, TO POUR OUT, Isa. 29:10, specially—
(1) in honour of a god, *to make a libation*, σπένδειν, Ex. 30:9; Hos. 9:4. Whence Isa. 30:1, נְסֹךְ מַסֵּכָה σπένδεσθαι σπονδήν, to make a covenant, because the ancients in making covenants were accustomed to offer libations, (compare Lat. *spondere*, from σπονδή).
(2) *to cast* out of metal, Isa. 40:19; 44:10.
(3) *to anoint* a king, Psalm 2:6. Compare נָסִיךְ No. 3.
NIPHAL pass. of No. 3, *to be anointed*, Prov. 8:23.
PIEL i. q. Kal No. 1, *to make a libation*, 1 Chr. 11:18; Syr. Pa. id. In the parallel place, 2 Sam. 23:16; there is Hiph. which is more used in the older Hebrew.
HIPHIL id. *to pour out* (libations), *to make a libation*, Gen. 35:14; Num. 28:7; Jer. 7:18; Psalm 16:4.
HOPHAL pass. Ex. 25:29; 37:16.
Derived nouns, נֶסֶךְ, נָסִיךְ No. I. מַסֵּכָה.
II. נָסַךְ i. q. cogn. סָכַךְ.—(1) TO INTERTWINE, TO WEAVE, TO HEDGE, i. q. Arab. نسج, hence מַסֶּכֶת the warp. From the idea of hedging, fencing, comes—
(2) *to cover, to protect*, Isa. 25:7. Comp. מַסֵּכָה No. II.

נֶסֶךְ and נֵסֶךְ m. with suff. נִסְכִּי, pl. נְסָכִים, נִסְכֵּי.
(1) *a libation, a drink-offering*, Gen. 35:14; Jer. 7:18; מִנְחָה וָנֶסֶךְ the offering (without blood), and the drink-offering, Joel 1:9.

נְסַךְ emphat. st. נִסְכָּא Chald. *a libation, a drink-offering*, Ezr. 7:17 [plur. with suff. נִסְכֵּיהוֹן].
נִסְמָן see סָמַן.
I. נָסַס i. q. מָסַס TO PINE AWAY, TO BE SICK (Syr. [Syriac] Ethpa. id. [Syriac] sick. Compare Hebr. נוּשׁ, אָנַשׁ). Isa. 10:18, כִּמְסֹס נֹסֵס "as a sick man pines away" [But a very good sense is given in English version, in which it is taken as from the following].
II. נָסַס not used in Kal, i. q. נָשָׂא ["Arabic نص"] prop. TO LIFT UP, TO EXALT; whence נֵס.
HITHPAEL, Zech. 9:16, "they shall be (as) אַבְנֵי נֵזֶר מִתְנוֹסְסוֹת עַל־אַדְמָתוֹ the stones of a diadem lifting themselves up in his land." But הִתְנוֹסֵם Ps. 60:6, is from the root נוּס.
נָסַע—(1) prop. i. q. Arab نزع TO PULL UP, TO PLUCK OUT (compare נָסַח); e. g. door posts, Jud. 16:3, 14; especially the stakes of a tent when a camp moves, Isa. 33:20. Hence—
(2) *to remove a camp, to break up* from an encampment (as a nomadic band), Gen. 35:16; 37:17; Num. 10:18; 33:3, seqq; an army of soldiers, Exod. 14:10; 2 Ki. 19:8; and also—
(3) *to remove, to depart* (aufbrechen); e. g. used of the angel of God, Exod. 14:19; also used of the ark of the covenant, Num. 10:33; of a wind springing up, Num. 11:31; *to migrate, to journey*, often used of nomadic tribes, Gen. 12:9; 33:17.
(4) *to bend a bow*; Arab. نزع; see מַסָּע.
NIPHAL, *to be plucked out*, used of the cords of a tent, Job 4:21 (see under the word יֶתֶר); of a tent itself, Isa. 38:12.
