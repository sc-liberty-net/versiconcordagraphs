לַהֲשׁוֹת 2 Kings 19:25, contr. for לְהַשְׁאוֹת Isaiah 37:26, inf. Hiphil, from שָׁאָה.
לַחְמָם Isa. 47:14, inf. in pause, from the root חָמַם, of the form קְטָל; compare לַחֲנַנְכֶם Isa. 30:18.
לִיסֹד[?] 2 Ch. 31:7, inf. Kal, from יָסַד, for the common לִיסוֹד (Isa. 51:16), from the root יָסַד.
לִיקְּהַת[?] for לְיִקֲהַת[?] by a Syriacism, from the noun יְקָהָה[?]. Prov. 30:17.
לֵךְ imp. from יָלַךְ to go; see הָלַךְ.
לְכָה, לֵךְ see in its place, p. cccxxxix, A.
לֶכֶת inf. f. Kal, from יָלַךְ; see הָלַךְ.
לָנֶה for לָנָה *she passed the night*; 3 fem. pret. from לוּן Zec. 5:4
לַצְבּוֹת contr. for לְהַצְבּוֹת inf. Hiphil, from the root צָבָה.
לֵרֹא for לִירֹא inf. Kal, from יָרֵא to fear.
לַת, with prep. לְ, לָלַת, inf. Kal, contracted from לֶדֶת, from יָלַד to bring forth, Lehrg. 133.

מֵבִי part. Hiphil, for מֵבִיא from the root בּוֹא.
מְבַעִתֶּךָ for מְבַעִתְּךָ part. Piel, fem., from בָּעַת Piel, to frighten, 1 Sam. 16:15.
מִבָּרִאשֹׁנָה 1 Ch. 15:13; comp. of מָה, בָּ, רִאשֹׁנָה *that which (is) of old*. See מָה note, let. *c*.
מִדִּין Jud. 5:10, plur. from מַד; Chald. form.
מְהֵימַן part. pass. Aphel, Chald. from the root אֲמַן.
מְהַחֲתִין[?] part. Aphel, Chald. from נְחַת to go down.
מְהָךְ inf. Peal, Chald. from the root הוּךְ to go.
מוּעָדֶת part. Kal, for מוֹעֶדֶת from מָעַד to nod; which see.
מוּמָת part. Hophal, from מוּת to die.
מוּסָב part. Hophal, from סָבַב to surround.
מוּצָא part. Hophal, from יָצָא to go out.
מוֹרִינִים[?] pl. from מוֹרַג a threshing wain, for מוֹרַגִּים. Lehrg. 145.
מֵזֵא with suff מֵזְיֵהּ inf. Peal, Chald. from the root אֲזָה to kindle.
מַזֶּה *what is this?* for מַה־זֶּה, see מָה note.
מֵזִין for מַאֲזִין part. Hiphil, from אָזַן No. I.
מַחֵא part. Aphel Ch. from חֲיָא to live.
מִחַן inf. Peal Ch. from חֲנַן.
מַחֲצֹצְרִים, see חָצֵר.
מְטַהֵר part. Hithpael, from טָהַר, for מִתְטַהֵר.
מְלָאכִים 2 Sa. 11:1, i.q. מְלָכִים (which is also the reading of 31 MSS. and 7 early editions) *kings*; with a redundant א *mater lectionis*.
מָלוּ for מָלְאוּ pret. Kal, from the root מָלֵא.
מַלִּינִים part. Hiphil, from לוּן note 2, with a Rabbinic inflexion. Lehrg. 407.
מַלֵּף for מְאַלֵּף part. Piel; from אָלַף to teach; Syriac form.
מַלְתִּי for מָלֵאתִי pret. Kal from מָלֵא.
=== RIGHT COLUMN ===
מִמְּךָ m. מִמֵּךְ f. *from thee*, from מִן, which see.
מִנְּזָרַיִךְ Nah. 3:17, from מִנְזָרִים.
מֻנָּח part. Hophal, letter B, from נוּחַ.
מַנִּיחַ part. Hiphil, letter B, of the same verb.
מְעֻזְנֶיהָ[?] for מָעֻזֶּיהָ[?] Isa. 23:11. This form (which many critics have been inclined, without any need, to alter) follows the same analogy as has been above explained (page cc, B), under דָּלַל, which is also more widely extended. The following examples may be added to those above given. פִּלְנְאָסֶר[?] for פִּלְאָסֶר[?], σμύρρα, σμύρνα; φύλλον, *folium* (*foljum*); ἄλλος, *alius*; and perhaps also עָזְנִיָּה (a species of eagle), for עֻזִּיָּה i.e. strong, robust; and even עֻזִּיָּה and עזריה (pr. n. Uzziah).
מֵעַל comp. of מִן and עַל, see עַל.
מֵעִם comp. of מִן and עִם, pr. von bey, *de chez*, see עִם.
מֹצַאֲכֶם Gen. 32:20, for מָצַאֲכֶם, inf. Kal, from מָצָא. Compare הַעֲלֹה[?] for הֶעָלָה[?].
מְקַלְלַוְנִי Jer. 15:10, a form apparently compounded of two readings (מְקַלְלִנִי and קִלְלוּנִי), which must rather be ascribed to the copyists than to the writer.
מְקֻנַּנְתִּי Jer. 22:23 כתיב, part. Pual, fem. from קָנַן to make a nest, with Yod paragogic, which in קרי is omitted.
מְקַרְקַר part. Piel, from קוּר.
מִשְׁתַּחֲוִיתֶם Ezek. 8:16, probably an error of the author [but *he* was inspired] or copyist for מִשְׁתַּחֲוִים *those worshipping*. Some thought the termination תֶּם to be shortened from אַתֶּם *you*, so that that form was contracted from this word and from the part. (in the Syriac form); but it could hardly be the *second* person, because of the preceding הֵמָּה. It is, however, so laid down by Ewald, Crit. Gram. page 489.
מַתָּ, מַתָּה *thou hast died*, pret. Kal, from מוּת.
מֵתָא inf. Peal Ch. from אֲתָה to come.

נ

נָאוֹת see יֵאֹתוּ[?].
נֵאשַׁאר[?] Eze. 9:8, a form compounded of two readings, which are actually found in MSS. and printed editions (נִשְׁאַר and אֶשָּׁאֵר).
נָבְזָה 1 Sa. 14:36, for נָבֹזָּה fut. Kal, from בָּזַז, with Dagesh rejected.
נִבֵּיתִי for נִבֵּאתִי pret. Niphal, from נָבָא to prophesy.
נָבֵל, וַנָּבֶל *we fade*, Isaiah 64:5, for וַנִּבָּל[?], from the root נָבֵל.
נָבְלָה for נָבֹלָה fut. Kal, from בָּלַל. § 66, 5, and note 11. Lehrg. 372.
נִבְקָה[?] for נָבֹקָה[?] pret. Niphal, from בָּקַק ibid.
נִגְאָלוּ Isa. 59:3; Lam. 4:14; see Niphal גָּאַל No. II.
נִדְמָה Jer. 8:14, for נִדַּמָּה[?] fut. Niphal, from דָּמַם No. II. Compare § 66, 5, and note 11.
נְהִי, וַנְּהִי 1 pl. fut. apoc. Kal, from הָיָה to be.
