בָּתַת an unused root. Arabic بت I. IV. to cut, to cut off, to break off (comp. under the root בָּדַד No. I.); بتّ something broken off, destroyed; بتة
ג[?]
Gimel (גִּימֶל), the third letter of the alphabet, when used as a numeral, i. q. *three*. Its name differs only in form from גָּמָל *camel*; and its figure in the Phœnician monuments (4, ٦)[?], on the coins of the Maccabees, and in the Æthiopic alphabet (ገ)[?], bears a resemblance to the neck of the camel. The Greeks received this letter from the Phœnicians, and by turning the head to the right, made it Γ.
As being the softest of the palatals (גיכק) except Yod, it is often *interchanged* with the harder ones כ and ק; both within the limits of the Hebrew language itself, and as found by a comparison with cognate languages, see גָּדִישׁ, كديس, جدس a heap of sheaves; גָּפְרִית, ܟܒܪܝܬܐ[?], كبريت sulphur; גָּנַן and כָּנַן to cover, to protect; גָּנַז and כָּנַס, كنز to collect, to heap up; רָכַל and רָגַל to run up and down; גְּבִיעַ, قمعة calix of flowers; גָּדַד, جد and قد to cut; שָׁקַד, שְׁקֵד[?], ܫܓܕܐ[?] almond.
More rarely it passes over—(2) into *gutturals*, which are less allied; namely, ע see גָּרָה, גָּמַץ[?], and ח, as אֶפְרֹחַ, ܦܪܘܓܐ[?] young of birds, comp. אָחַח[?].
גֵּא adj. (for גֵּאֶה, from the root גָּאָה), m. *proud, arrogant*, Isa. 16:6.
גָּאָה fut. יִגְאֶה a poetical word.

Derivatives follow, except גֵּא, גֵּוָה No. II.
גֵּאֶה adj.—(1) *lifted up, high*, Isa. 2:12. Job 40:11, 12, רְאֵה כָל־גֵּאֶה וְהַשְׁפִּילֵהוּ " behold every thing that is high, and bring it low."
(2) *proud, arrogant*, Jer. 48:29. Pl. גֵּאִים the proud, often with the adjoined notion of impiety; as elsewhere, meekness and a humble spirit include the idea of piety (see עָנָו). Psa. 94:2; 140:6; Pro 15:25; 16:19. LXX. ὑπερήφανοι, ὑβρισταί.
גֵּאָה f. *pride, arrogance*, Pro. 8:13.
גְּאוּאֵל ("majesty of God"), [*Geuel*], pr. n. m. Nu. 13:15.
גַּאֲוָה f. pr. elevation (from גָּאָה), hence—
(1) *magnificence, majesty*, as of God, Deu. 33:26; Ps. 68:35.
(2) *ornament, splendour* (Pracht), Job 41:7; Deu. 33:29.
(3) *pride, arrogancy*. Psa. 73:6, לָכֵן עֲנָקַתְמוֹ גַאֲוָה " therefore pride clothes their neck," i. e. they are elated with pride. A stiff neck being regarded as the seat of pride. Ps. 31:24; Pro. 14:3; Isa. 9:8; 13:3, 11; 16:6; 25:11. Used of the sea, Psa. 46:4, " the mountains quake at its pride."
גְּאוּלִים m. plur. (of the form קְדוּמִים, עֲשׁוּקִים), *redemptions, redemption*. Isa. 63:4, שְׁנַת גְּאוּלַי "the year of my redemption," i. e. in which I will redeem my people. So LXX., Vulg, Syr. Commonly taken as "the year of my redeemed ones."
גָּאוֹן const. גְּאוֹן, once pl. גְּאוֹנִים (from the root גָּאָה), Eze. 16:56, pr. elevation; hence—
(1) *sublimity, majesty*, of God, Ex. 15:7; Isa. 2:10, 19, 21; 24:14, בִּגְאוֹן יְיָ צָהֲלוּ " they sing with joy of the majesty of Jehovah." Job 37:4, קוֹל גְּאוֹנוֹ " his sublime voice," thunder. Job 40:10, עֲדֵה־נָא גָאוֹן וָגֹבַהּ " deck thyself, now, with majesty and magnificence." Mic. 5:3.
