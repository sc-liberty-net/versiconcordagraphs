Derivatives, the following nouns and מוֹרָד.

יֶרֶד ("descent"), [*Jared*], pr. n. m.—(1) Gen. 5:15. Gr. Ἰαρέδ, Luke 3:37.—(2) 1 Ch. 4:18.

יַרְדֵּן in prose always with the art. הַיַּרְדֵּן (as an appellative, river, so called from descending, flowing down; like the Germ. Rhyn, Rhein, from the verb rinnen, compare [Arabic] a lake, sea), *Jordan*, Gr. ὁ Ἰορδάνης, the largest river of Palestine, rising at the foot of Antilibanus, and flowing into the Dead Sea, where it is lost, Gen. 13:10, 11; 32:11; 50:10. Arab. [Arabic] *el-Urdun*, and also now it is called [Arabic] *esh-Sheri'ah*, i. e. *the ford* (as having been of old crossed by the Israelites), [or rather, *the watering place*]. אֶרֶץ יַרְדֵּן the country near Jordan, Ps. 42:7.—Job 40:23, Jordan is put for any large river [?] (like Cicero, *a Cicero*, for a great orator); and this marks a writer as belonging to Palestine: [not necessarily, any more than such a use of the name Cicero would mark a writer to be a Roman].

יָרָה inf. יְרֹה, יְרוֹת, and יָרוֹא 2 Chron. 26:15, imp. יְרֵה, fut. יִירֶה [1 pers. pl. with suff. נִירָם].
(1) TO CAST, Ex. 15:4; e.g. a lot, Josh. 18:6; an arrow, 1 Sa. 20:36, 37; Ps. 11:2; 64:5; Num. 21:30. Part. יוֹרֶה an archer, 1 Ch. 10:3.
(2) *to lay foundations* (compare Gr. βάλλεσθαι ὄστυ [?], for to lay the foundations of a city; Syr. [Syriac] to cast, to place a foundation), *to found*. Job 38:6, "who laid (or founded, or placed) its corner stone." Gen. 31:51, "behold the pillar אֲשֶׁר יָרִיתִי which I have founded," i. e. placed, or raised.
(3) *to sprinkle*, *to water* (pr. to throw water, to scatter drops, compare זָרַק), Hos. 6:3. Hence part. יוֹרֶה the former rain, see above, page CCCXLIII, B.
NIPHAL, pass. of No. 1, *to be shot through with darts* or *arrows*. Fut. יִיָּרֶה Ex. 19:13.
HIPHIL הוֹרָה, fut. יוֹרֶה, with Vav convers. וַיּוֹר.
(1) i. q. Kal, *to cast*, Job 30:19; specially arrows, 1 Sa. 20:20, 36. Fut. apoc. וַיּוֹר 2 Ki. 13:17. Part. מוֹרֶה *an archer*, 1 Sam. 31:3. Written by an Aramæism in the manner of verbs לא, 2 Sam. 11:24, וַיֹּרְאוּ הַמּוֹרְאִים "and the archers shot," comp. 2 Ch. 26:15.

(2) *to sprinkle, to water*. Part. מוֹרֶה [?] i. q. יוֹרֶה "the former rain," Joel 2:23; Ps. 84:7.
(3) *to send out the hand* (like שָׁלַח יָד), especially for pointing out. Hence *to show, to indicate*, Gen. 46:28. Prov. 6:13, מֹרֶה בְּאֶצְבְּעֹתָיו "showing (i. e. giving signals) with his fingers;" followed by two accusatives, of the person and of the thing, Ex. 15:25.
From pointing out or shewing it is—(4) *to teach, to instruct* (comp. Gr. δεικνύω, ἀναφαίνω), Ex. 35:34; Mic. 3:11; followed by an acc. of pers. *to instruct* any one, Job 6:24; 8:10; 12:7, 8; followed by an acc. of the thing, Isa. 9:14; Hab. 2:18. Hence followed by two accusatives, of the person and of the thing, Ps. 27:11; 86:11; 119:33; followed by בְּ of the thing (properly to instruct in something), Job 27:11; Psa. 25:8, 12; 32:8; more rarely followed by אֶל (prop. to teach or conform *to* something) 2 Ch. 6:27; or מִן (to instruct *concerning*) Isa. 2:3; Mic. 4:2. Followed by a dative of pers. and acc. of the thing, Deu. 33:10; Hos. 10:12
Derived nouns, תּוֹרָה, מוֹרֶה, יוֹרֶה [also יוֹרַי, יוֹרָה, יְרִיָּה, יְרִיאֵל, יְרוּשָׁלַיִם, יְרוּאֵל].

יָרָהּ ἅπαξ λεγόμ., in my judgment, kindred to the roots יָרֵא, יָרַע, pr. TO TREMBLE; hence *to be frightened*, like the Arabic [Arabic]. Hence תִּרְהוּ (2 Codd. תִּירְהוּ), Isa. 44:8, i. q. תִּשְׁמוּ [?]. LXX. μὴ πλανᾶσθε. All the other versions express the signification of fearing, as though it were the same as תִּירָאוּ.

יְרוּאֵל ("people," or "habitation of God," see יְרוּשָׁלַיִם ["founded by God"]), [*Jeruel*], pr. n. ["of a town and"] desert, 2 Ch. 20:16.

יָרוֹחַ (i. q. יָרֵחַ "moon"), [*Jaroah*], pr. name, m. 1 Ch. 5:14.

יָרוֹק m. *that which is green*, Job 39:8. Root יָרַק.

יְרוּשָׁה & יְרוּשָׁא ("possessed, sc. by a husband"), [*Jerusha*], pr. n. of the mother of king Jotham, 2 Ki. 15:33; 2 Ch. 27:1.
