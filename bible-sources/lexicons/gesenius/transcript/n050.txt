RUNNING HEADS: אחזי—אחיקם | XXXI | אחז—אחיקם [?]

אַחְזַי [*Ahasai*], pr.n. of a man, Neh. 11:13, for which there is in 1 Ch. 9:12 יַחְזְרָה. Perhaps we should read in both places אֲחַזְיָה.

אֲחַזְיָה & אֲחַזְיָהוּ [?] ("whom Jehovah upholds"), pr.n.—(1) *Ahaziah*, king of Israel, son of Ahab and Jezebel (B.C. 897—895), 1 Ki. 22:40; 2 Ki. 1:2. LXX. Ὀχυζίας.—(2) *Ahaziah*, son of Jehoram, king of Judah (B.C. 884), 2 Ki 8:24; 9:16.

אֲחֻזָּם ("their possession"), [*Ahuzam*], pr.n. of a man, a descendant of Judah, 1 Ch. 4:6.

אֲחֻזַּת ("possession"), [*Ahuzzath*], pr.n. of a Philistine, a friend of king Abimelech, Gen. 26:26.

אָחַח an unused root.— I. Arab. [Arabic] onomatop. from the sound אָח, *to cry out ah! repeatedly*. In Hebrew perhaps, *to groan* (ächzen), *to howl*, whence אֹחִים. The Arabs have under the same root —
II. the signification of *heat, burning, anger*, in the word [Arabic], [Arabic], whence, perhaps, may be derived אָח, [Arabic] *a pot, a furnace*. I had rather however take the signification of furnace, from the root [Arabic] to burn, to kindle as fire; Conj. II. to set on fire, [Arabic] heat, etc. See ב [?].

אֵחִי [*Ehi*], see אֵחוּד.

אֲחִי (perhaps contracted from אֲחִיָּה), [*Ahi*], pr.n. m.—(1) 1 Ch. 5:15;—(2) 1 Ch. 7:34.

אֲחִיאָם (for אֲחִיאָב "father's brother"), [*Ahiam*], pr.n. m., 2 Sa. 23:33; 1 Ch. 11:33.

אֲחִידָה Ch. i. q. Heb. חִידָה, with Aleph prosthetic, *an enigma*, Dan. 5:12. Root חוד.

אֲחִיָּה ("brother," i.e. "friend of Jehovah"), [*Ahiah, Ahijah*], pr.n.—(1) of a certain priest in the time of Saul, 1 Sa. 14:3, 8. — (2) 1 Ch. 8:7. — (3) 1 Ch. 11:36.—(4) 1 Ki. 4:3.—(5) 1 Ch. 26:20. —(6) 1 Ch. 2:25.—(7) 1 Ki. 15:27, 33.—(8) Neh. 10:27.—(9) a prophet living at Shiloh in the time of Jeroboam, 1 Ki. 11:29; 12:15; called אֲחִיָּהוּ 1 Ki. 14:6, 18; 2 Ch. 10:15.

אֲחִיהוּד ("brother," i.e. "friend of the Jews," for אֲחִי יְהוּד), [*Ahihud*], pr.n. m. Nu. 34:27.

אַחְיוֹ ("brotherly"), [*Ahio*], pr.n. m. — (1) 2 Sa. 6:3, 4.—(2) 1 Ch. 8:14.—(3) 1 Ch. 8:31; 9:37.

=== RIGHT COLUMN ===

אֲחִיחוּד ("brother," or "friend of union"), [*Ahihud*], pr.n. m. 1 Ch. 8:7.

אֲחִיטוּב ("brother," or "friend of goodness"), [*Ahitub*], pr.n. m.—(1) 1 Sa. 14:3; 22:9 —(2) 2 Sa. 8:17.— (3) 1 Ch. 5:37; Neh. 11:11.

אֲחִילוּד ("brother of one born," for אֲחִי יָלוּד), [*Ahilud*], pr.n. m. of the father of Jehoshaphat, 2 Sa. 8:16; 20:24; 1 Ki. 4:3.

אֹחִים; see אָח.

אֲחִימוֹת ("brother of death"), [*Ahimoth*], pr.n. m. 1 Ch. 6:10; for which there is in the parallel places מַחַת.

אֲחִימֶלֶךְ ("brother of the king"), [*Ahimelech*], pr.n.—(1) a priest living at Nob, the father of Abiathar, the intimate friend of David (1 Sa. 21:2; 22:9; Ps. 52:2), and therefore slain by Saul. Different from this apparently may be — (2) *Ahimelech*, the son of Abiathar, one of the two high priests in the time of David, 2 Sa. 8:17; 1 Ch. 24:3, 6, 31. Korb, however (Winer Theol. Journal IV. p. 295), supposes, with a great deal of probability, that in 2 Sa. 8:17, for "Ahimelech, the son of Abiathar," we should read, "Abiathar, the son of Ahimelech;" from this erroneous reading he supposes that a mistake was introduced into the Chronicles.

אֲחִימָן ("brother of gift"), [*Ahiman*], pr.n. m.—(1) one of the Anakim, Nu. 13:22; Josh. 15:14, Jud. 1:10.—(2) 1 Ch. 9:17.

אֲחִימַעַץ ("brother of anger"), [*Ahimaaz*], pr.n. m.—(1) 1 Sa. 14:50.—(2) the son of Zadok, the high priest in the time of David, 2 Sa. 15:27, 36; 17:17, 20; 18:19, seq. It appears to be the same who is mentioned, 1 K. 4:15.

אַחְיָן [?] ("brotherly"), [*Ahian*], pr.n. m., 1 Ch. 7:19.

אֲחִינָדָב ("liberal," or "noble brother") [*Ahinadab*], pr.n. m., 1 Ki. 4:14.

אֲחִינֹעַם ("brother of grace"), [*Ahinoam*] pr.n. f. — (1) 1 Sa. 14:50. — (2) 1 Sa. 25:43; 27:3, 30:5; 2 Sa. 2:2; 3:2.

אֲחִיסָמָךְ ("brother of support," or "aid"), [*Ahisamach*], pr.n. m., Ex. 31:6; 35:34.

אֲחִיעֶזֶר ("brother of aid"), [*Ahiezer*], pr.n. m.—(1) a captain of the Danites, Nu. 1:12; 2:25; 7:66.—(2) 1 Ch. 12:3.
