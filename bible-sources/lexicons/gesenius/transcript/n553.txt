Derivatives נְדָבָה, נָדִיב, and the pr. n. נָדָב, נוֹדָב.

נְדַב Chald. Ithpael i. q. Hebr.—(1) *to be willing, ready* for anything; followed by לְ Ezr. 7:13.
(2) *to give freely*, ibid. verse 15. Inf. (in the Syriac manner) הִתְנַדָּבוּת subst. *a free-will offering*, verse 16.

נָדָב ("spontaneous," "liberal"), [*Nadab*], pr. n.—(1) of the son of Jeroboam I. king of the Ten Tribes, 954—952, B. C., 1 Ki. 15:25, 31.—(2) a son of Aaron, Exod. 6:23; 24:1.—(3) 1 Ch. 2:28.—(4) ibid. 8:30; 9:36.

נְדָבָה f.—(1) *free-will, readiness of mind* (to give); whence בִּנְדָבָה Num. 15:3; Psalm 54:8; and acc. נְדָבָה Deut. 23:24; Hos. 14:5; *freely, with a willing mind*. Plur. Psa. 110:3, עַמְּךָ נְדָבוֹת "thy people are willingnesses;" i. e. very prompt for military service [?], abstr. for concr.
(2) *a spontaneous offering*, Ezr. 1:4 (compare verse 7), especially *a freewill sacrifice*, opp. to one that has been vowed (נֶדֶר); Exod. 35:29; Lev. 22:23, נְדָבָה תַּעֲשֶׂה אֹתוֹ "as a voluntary sacrifice thou mayest offer it." One who offers spontaneously, and with a willing mind, is not sparing but gives with a *large* hand; hence—
(3) *largeness, abundance*, Ps. 68:10, גֶּשֶׁם נְדָבוֹת "abundant, copious rain."

נְדַבְיָה ("whom Jehovah impels"), [*Nedabiah*], pr. n. m. 1 Ch. 3:18.

נִדְבָּךְ ἅπαξ λεγόμ. Ezr. 6:4; Chald. *a series of stones*, or *a wall*, both of which significations are found in the Targums (Eze. 46:33; Zec. 4:10), and in the Mishnah (t. i. page 7, 8; v. page 361; vi. p. 107, Surenh.) It seems to be derived from the Heb. נִדְבָּק part. Niph. prop. *joined together, cleaving together*.

נָדַד plur. נָדְדוּ; inf. נְדֹד; fut. יִדֹּד Nah. 3:7, and יִדַּד Gen. 31:40.
(1) trans. TO MOVE, e. g. the wings of a bird, Isa. 10:14. (Cogn. נוּד, נוּט; compare also Sanscr. *nat*, to move, to be moved.)
(2) intrans. *to move oneself*, hence, *to wander about*; used of a bird, Prov. 27:8; Isa. 16:2; used of men, Job 15:23. Part. נוֹדֵד *a wanderer, a fugitive*, Isa. 16:3; 21:14; Jer. 49:5.
(3) *to flee, to flee away*, Psalm 31:12; 55:8; 68:13; *to fly away* (used of a bird) Jerem. 4:25;

נְדֻדִים m. plur. *unquiet motions, tossings* of a sleepless man upon his bed, Job 7:4.

I. נָדָה not used in Kal, i. q. נָדַד TO FLEE, TO GO AWAY (Syr. and Samar. id.).
PIEL נִדָּה *to remove*, followed by לְ Amos 6:3; *to cast out, to exclude*, Isa. 66:5; compare נָדַד No. 4. (In the Rabbinic נִדּוּי is excommunication, separation from the congregation.)

II. נָדָה an unused root, i. q. נָדַב No. 2. to give freely, to be liberal (Arab. ندا to be moist, liberal), hence נָדָן and—

נֵדֶה masc. *a large gift*, given to a harlot, Eze. 16:33.

נִדָּה f. prop. *abomination* (see the root No. 4), *uncleanness, impurity*, Zec. 13:1; מֵי־הַנִּדָּה Num. 19:9, 13, 20, 21, water of impurity, i. e. water by which the unclean were purged, cleansing water. Specially—(1) *filth, menstrual uncleanness* of women, Levit. 12:2; 15:19, 20; hence used of the menstrual discharge, Lev. 15:24, 25, 33.
(2) *something unclean*, or *filthy*, i. q. תּוֹעֵבָה used of idols, 2 Ch. 29:5; Ezr. 9:11; Lam. 1:17; of incest, Lev. 20:21.

נָדַח fut. יִדַּח prop. TO THRUST (stoßen), TO IMPEL (see the cogn. דָּחָה and the observations made there); hence—
(1) *to thrust forth, to expel*, 2 Sam. 14:14; see HIPHIL.
(2) *to thrust against*, (as an axe against a tree), followed by עַל Deu. 20:19.
