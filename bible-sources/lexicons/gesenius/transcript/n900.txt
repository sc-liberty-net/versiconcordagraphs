יֻכַּת, יֻכַּתּוּ fut. Hophal, of a Chald. form, from כָּתַת, for יוּכַת, יוּכַּתּוּ, § 66, 5.
יַלִּיזוּ for יָלִיזוּ fut. Hiphil, from לוּז in the Rabbinic form.
וַיָּלִינוּ, יָלִינוּ fut. Hiphil, from לוּן, which see.
וַיִּמַח, יִמַח Gen. 7:23, fut. apoc. Kal, from—
מָחָה, but other good copies, both MSS. and printed (as that of V. D. Hooght), read יִמַּח fut. Niphal of the same verb.
יְמַלֵּה fut. Piel, from מָלֵא to be full, for יְמַלֵּא.
יֵמַר fut. A, from מָרַר to be bitter, for יִמְרַר. § 66, note 3. Lehrg. 366.
יֹמְרוּךָ for יֹאמְרוּךָ fut. Kal, from the root אָמַר Ps. 139:20.
יְנָאֵץ[?] fut. Hiphil, in the Syr. form for יַנְאִיץ from נָאַץ. Lehrg. 411.
יִנְדַּע fut. Peal Ch. from יְדַע to know, for יִדַּע; Dagesh forte resolved into Nun. Compare מַנְדַּע for מַדַּע.
יָנִי fut. Hiphil, from the root נוּא, for יָנִיא. Ps. 141:5.
יַנִּיחַ fut. Hiphil, B. from the root נוּחַ.
יִסֹּב fut. Kal, in the Ch. manner for יָסֹב, from סָבַב.
יַסֵּב fut. Hiphil, from סָבַב, in the Ch. manner for יָסֵב.
יָעֵד, וַיָּעַד fut. Hiphil, from the root עוּד.
יָעַט, וַיָּעַט, and (with Dagesh forte occult), וַיַּעַט 1 Sa. 25:14, and 14:32 קרי, fut. apoc. Kal, from the root עִיט.
יַעֲמֹדְנָה 3 fut. fem. pl. for תַּעֲמֹדְנָה. § 47, note 3. Lehrg. 276.
יְעֹעֲרוּ Isaiah 15:5, for יְעַרְעֲרוּ *they arouse* (a clamour); the letter *r* being softened into a vowel (see Ewald's Crit. Gr. page 479), unless we should read יערערו; whence יעוערו, defectively יְעֹעֲרוּ.
יָעֵר, וַיָּעַר fut. Hiphil, from the root עוּר.
יֶפֶר fut. Hiphil apoc. from פָּרָה, for יַפְרֶה.
יַפְתְּ fut. Hiphil apoc. from פָּתָה.
יִצְטַבַּע fut. Ithpael, Chald. from the root צְבַע.
יִצְטַיְּרוּ see צִיר Hithpael.
יֵצֶק, יִצֹּק fut. Kal, from יָצַק to pour, which see.
יֵצֶר and יִצֹּר he shall form; fut. Kal, from יָצַר to form.
וַיָּצַר, יָצַר fut. apoc. from צוּר.
וַיֵּצֶר, יֵצֶר fut. Kal, from יָצַר No. II.
יִצַּתּוּ fut. Kal, from יָצַת to kindle, in pause with Dagesh euphon.
יִקַּח fut. Kal, and יֻקַּח fut. Hophal, from לָקַח, for יִלָּקַח, יֻלְקַח.
יַקֵּץ 1 Ki. 3:15, in some copies and editions for יִיקַץ, from יָקַץ.
יִקְרֶךָ[?] 1 Sa. 28:10, for יִקְּרֶךָ[?] (with Dag. euphon.) fut. Kal from קָרָה, with suffix ֶךָ.
יָקֹשׁ, וַיָּקָשׁ fut. apoc. Kal, from the root קוֹשׁ.
יָקֵשׁ fut. apoc. Hiphil, of the same verb.
יֵרֶא, וַיֵּרֶא fut. apoc. Kal, from רָאָה to see.
יְראוּ for the common יִירְאוּ imp. from יָרֵא (to fear), Ps. 34:10, in the manner of verbs לה, so inflected that א is otiose; compare נִרְפְּאוּ Eze. 47:8; Lehrg. 417.
יִרְאוּ (*they fear*), for יִירְאוּ; it differs from יִרְאוּ (with short Chirek), *they see*.
יָרֶב, וַיִּרֶב for וַיֶּאֱרֹב[?] fut. Hiphil, from אָרַב to lay wait.

יִשַּׁרְנָה 1 Sam. 6:12, 3 fem. plur. Kal, for תִּשַּׁרְנָה, from יָשַׁר. § 47, note 3.
יִשְׁתַּחוּ, וַיִּשְׁתַּחוּ fut. apoc. sing. Hithpal., from יִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה, from the root שָׁחָה.
יִשְׁתַּקְשְׁקוּ fut. Hithpalpel, from שָׁקַק.
