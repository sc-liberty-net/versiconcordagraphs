RUNNING HEADS: שאט־תחי | DCCCLXXXIV | תחל־תתצב

שׁ

שָׁאט part. from שׁוּט No. II., to reject.
שַׁבְתִּי (Milrâ) Ps. 23:6, commonly, *my dwelling*, for שִׁבְתִּי inf. with suff. from יָשַׁב. But it seems to have been rather regarded by the authors of the points as by aphæresis for יָשַׁבְתִּי. Compare שׁוֹב, רַד.
שָׁנָם see under שָׁנַן.
שׁוֹב for יָשׁוֹב inf pleonast. from יָשַׁב Jer. 42:10. Compare רַד, תַּתָּה.
שׁוֹשֵׂתִי pret. Poel, from שָׁסָה=שָׁשָׂה, which see.
שַׁמּוֹת inf. pl. from שָׁמֵם to lay waste, Eze. 36:3; Lehrg. 365.
שְׁנָתַיִם Dual, from שָׁנָה a year.
שַׁתִּי, שַׁתָּה, שָׁת pret. from שִׁית to place.

ת

תְּאֵהֲבוּ Proverbs 1:22, fut. Kal for תֶּאֱהָבוּ, the vowels being contracted in the Aramean manner, compare מְלֶךְ, מֶלֶךְ.
תְּאָכְלֵהוּ Job 20:26; fut. Piel for תְּאַכְּלֵהוּ from אָכַל.
תֹּאֲרוּ for תָּאֲרוּ, from תָּאַר. Compare פְּעֳלוֹ[?].
תֹּבֵא Pro. 1:10; a Chald. form for תֹּאבֶה[?], תֵּבֶה[?] fut. Kal from אָבָה to will.
תְּבֹאֶינָה[?] 3 plur. fut. fem. Kal, from בּוֹא to come, Ps. 45:16.
תְּבוֹאָתָה Deut. 33:16; for תָּבֹאָה; ה parag. is apparently put twice, and in like manner—
תְּבֹאָתְךָ Job 22:21, for תָּבֹאֲךָ and תָּבֹאתִי; תָּבֹאת קרי for תָּבֹאִי 1 Sam. 25:34. Others (as Ewald, Crit. Gr., p. 488) suppose the afformatives of the preterite to be added to the future; so that תְּבֹאָתָה would be from תָּבֹא and בָּאתָה (but the sense demands בָּאָה), תְּבֹאָתְךָ from תָּבֹאֲךָ and בָּאתָ, תְּבֹאתִי from תָּבֹאִי and בָּאת or בָּאתִי. Compare Lehrg. 464.
תֵּבְךְּ, וַתֵּבְךְּ fut. apoc. from בָּכָה to weep.
תִּגְבְּהֶינָה for תִּגְבַּהְנָה Eze. 16:50; the syllable ־ֶי being inserted in the manner of verbs לה and עע, on account of the similar sound of the word תַּעֲשֶׂינָה.
תֹּנְיוּן[?] fut. Hiphil, from יָנָה
תִּגָּל 2 fut. Niphal apoc. from גָּלָה.
תְּדַמְיוּן[?] fut. Piel from דָּמָה, for תְּדַמּוּנִי, the third radical י being preserved.
תְּהִי, וַתְּהִי in pause תֶּהִי, fut. Kal apoc. from הָיָה to be.
תְּהִימֶנָה for תֶּהֱמֶינָה[?] Mic. 2:12. Lehrg. 405.
תְּהִלָּתֵיךְ[?] for תְּהִלָּתֶךָ thy praise, redundant mater lectionis. See שְׂנֵאתַיִךְ.
תְּהָתֵלּוּ see the root התל.
תּוֹמִיךְ Ps. 16:5; partic. form of a rare kind, for תּוֹמֵךְ *holding*. Lehrg. 308.
תֵּזְלִי for תֶּאְזְלִי fut. Kal, from אָזַל to depart, Lehrg. 377.
תַּזְרֵנִי 2 Sam. 22:40; i. q. תְּאַזְּרֵנִי (Ps. 18:40) fut. Piel, from אָזַר to gird.
תֹּחֵז for תֹּאחֵז 2 fut. Kal from אָחַז to hold.
תְּחִי fut. apoc. Kal, from חָיָה to live.
=== RIGHT COLUMN ===
תֵּחַל fut. Niphal, from חָלַל profane, to pollute, Lev. 21:9.
תָּחַשׁ[?], וַתָּחַשׁ for וַתָּחִישׁ[?] (*it will hasten*) from the root חוּשׁ as to which analogy, see under the verb עִיט p. 621, Job 31:5.
תֵּט fut. apoc. Kal, from נָטָה for תִּטֶּה.
תִּיעָשֶׂה[?] Exodus 25:31; in very many editions for תֵּעָשֶׂה (although irregular, and almost a corruption), Lehrg. 52, 331.
תֵּכַהּ, וַתֵּכַהּ fut. Kal apoc., for תִּכְהֶה, from the root כָּהָה.
תְּכַס fut. apoc. Piel for תְּכַסֶּה from כָּסָה to cover.
תֵּלַהּ, וַתֵּלַהּ fut. apoc. Kal, from לָאָה=לָהָה. See this root.
תָּלַן fut. Kal, shortened from תָּלִין, from לוּן or לִין.
תֶּמְחִי for תַּמְחֶה 2 masc. (the letter י belongs to the root) fut. Hiph. from מָחָה to wipe away, Jer. 18:23.
תֶּמֶס, וַתֶּמֶס fut. apoc. Hiphil, from מָסָה to flow down.
תַּמֵּר Exod. 23:21; fut. Hiphil, Chaldee form for תָּמֵר, from מָרַר, with a signification taken from מָרָה to be stubborn
תִּמְרוּ for תֹּאמְרוּ fut. Kal, from אָמַר to speak.
תֵּן, תְּנָה imp. from נָתַן to give
תִּנְדַּע see יִנְדַּע.
תֹּסֵף[?] for תֹּאסֵף[?] fut. Kal, from אָסַף to collect.
תֵּעָגֵנָה for תֵּעָגַנָּה 2 plur. fut. Niphal, from עָגַן, dagesh left out, see under חָיָה.
תֹּפֵהוּ, וַתֹּפֵהוּ fut. Kal, from אָפָה to cook, for תֹּאפֵהוּ.
תְּפוֹצוֹתֵיכֶם Jer. 25:34; see the root פּוּץ.
תִּצַּלְנָה Jerem. 19:3; fut. Kal, from צָלַל, for the common תִּצְלֶינָה.
תראנה 1 Sa. 14:27 כתיב (not without an error), for תָּאֹרְנָה[?] The copyist appears to have had in his mind the word תִּרְאֶנָּה.
תֵּרֶב, וַתֵּרֶב fut. apoc. Kal, from רָבָה to be many, much.
תָּרוֹץ fut. Niphal, from רָצַץ to break, with the assumed form of a verb רוּץ.
תִּשֶּׂנָה Jer. 9:17; for תִּשֶּׂאנָה fut. Kal, from נָשָׂה.
תֶּשִׁי Deut. 32:18; see שָׁיָה.
תֵּשְׁתְּ fut. apoc. Kal, from שָׁתָה to drink.
תִּשְׁתַּחוּ fut. apoc. sing. Hithpa., from שָׁחָה to bow down.
תִּשְׁתָּע Isa. 41:10; fut. apoc. Hithpa., from שָׁעָה.
תֵּת inf. from נָתַן to give, contr. for תֶּנֶת. With suff. תִּתִּי
תִּתְבָּר 2 Sam. 22:27; for תִּתְבָּרַר fut. Hithp., from בָּרַר Lehrg. p. 374.
תַּתָּה by aphæresis for נָתַתָּה, which is the reading, Ps. 18:41 Compare רַד for יָרַד.
תִּתֹּם, תָּתֹם fut. Kal, from תָּמַם, which see.
תתן 1 Ki. 17:14 (כתיב); commonly taken for תֵּת (*to give*) with נ parag., like 6:19, where תִּתֵּן is really future. 1 Ki. loc. cit. תֵּת קרי seems preferable.
תֵּתַע, וַתֵּתַע fut. apoc. Kal, from תָּעָה.
תִּתְפַּל 2 Sam. 22:27; i. q תִּתְפַּתָּל, which is the reading, Ps 18:27. Lehrg. 374.
תִּתְצַב Ex. 2:4; for תִּתְיַצֵּב Hithpa., from יָצַב. Lehrg. 396
