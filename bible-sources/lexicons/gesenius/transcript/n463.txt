מַ pref. formed from מָה *what?* where see the note.
מִ pref. i. q. מִן, which see.
מָא Chald. i. q. Hebrew מָה *what?* and also without an interrogation, מָא דִי *that which*, Ezr. 6:8.
מַאֲבוּס m. *a stall*, for laying up fodder, *a storehouse*, Jer. 50:26; LXX. ἀποθήκη. Root אָבַס.
מְאֹד—(1) subst. m. *strength, force*, from the root אוד No. 3. Deu. 6:5, "and thou shalt love Jehovah thy God with all thy heart, with all thy mind, וּבְכֹל מְאֹדֶךָ and with all thy strength," i. e. in the highest degree; 2 Ki. 23:25 (compare Luke 10:27). Isaiah 47:9, בְּעָצְמַת חֲבָרַיִךְ מְאֹד "with (i. e. notwithstanding) the great might of thy sorceries," where we must join עָצְמַת מְאֹד; Job 35:15. Hence—(*a*) בִּמְאֹד מְאֹד with force of force, i. e. most forcibly, most violently, Gen. 17:2, 6, 20; Eze. 9:9.—(*b*) עַד מְאֹד even to force, i. e. in a very great degree; *very*, Genesis 27:33; 1 Ki. 1:4; Dan. 8:8; *quite, altogether*, Ps. 119:8; *too much*, Isa. 64:8.—(*c*) עַד לִמְאֹד, i. q. עַד מְאֹד but in the later Hebrew, 2 Chron. 16:14.
(2) adv.—(*a*) *very, exceedingly, greatly*, added to adjectives and verbs, as טוֹב מְאֹד Gen. 1:31; יָפָה מְאֹד 12:14; הַרְבֵּה מְאֹד 15:1. Emphatically doubled מְאֹד מְאֹד Gen. 7:19; Nu. 14:7.—Ps. 46:2, עֶזְרָה בְצָרוֹת נִמְצָא מְאֹד "(he is) an aid in distresses to be found greatly," i. e. he is found a strong aid.—(*b*) *quickly, hastily*, which is also connected with exertion of strength (compare Germ. bald, from the Lat. *valde*, and the Lower Germ. swieth, Anglo-Sax. *swithe*, very, Germ. geschwind), 1 Sam. 20:19, תֵּרֵד מְאֹד "go down quickly." Vulg. *festinus*.
מֵאָה constr. מְאַת f.—(1) A HUNDRED, Arab. مائة according to Kam., commonly مئة ["Æth. [Ethiopic], Syr. [Syriac]"]. It is prefixed to substantives in absol. and constr. state, מֵאָה שָׁנָה Gen. 17:17, and מְאַת שָׁנָה 25:7; it is more rarely put after them, and this is only in the later books; רִמּוֹנִים מֵאָה 2 Ch. 3:16. Dual מָאתַיִם (contr. for מִאְתַיִם) *two hundred*, Gen. 11:23. Pl. מֵאוֹת—(*a*) *hundreds* more than one; hence with other numerals שֵׁשׁ מֵאוֹת "six hundred," Ex. 12:37; שְׁמֹנֶה מֵאוֹת "eight hundred," Gen. 5:7, etc. Once—(*b*) *one hundred*, as if *centena*, 2 Ch.

(2) adv. *a hundred times*, Prov. 17:10; constr. מְאַת id. Ecc. 8:12.
(3) *the hundredth part*, i. e. of money or any thing, paid monthly, as being exacted in usury, Neh. 5:11, מְאַת הַכֶּסֶף וְהַדָּגָן. Vulg. *centesima*. As to the *centesima* of the Romans, see Ernesti Cl. Cic. h. v.; as to the heavy usury still customary in the East, see Volney's Travels, vol. ii. p. 410.
(4) [*Meah*], pr. n. of a tower at Jerusalem, Neh. 3:1; 12:39.
מְאָה Ch. id. Dan. 6:2; Ezr. 6:17; 7:22. Dual מָאתַיִן Ezr. 6:17.
מַאֲוַי once in pl. מַאֲוַיִּים m. *desires*, Psal. 140:9. Root אָוָה No. I.
מְאוּם for מְאוּם[?], commonly contr. מוּם (which see) *a spot, a blemish*. Dan. 1:4 [כ׳]; Job 31:7.
מְאוּמָה Milêl (from מָה וּמָה as if *quidquid*).—(1) *any thing whatever*, Num. 22:38; Deut. 24:10; 2 Ki. 5:20; with a particle of negation, לֹא מְאוּמָה *nothing*, Deut. 13:18; אֵין מְאוּמָה id. 1 Ki. 18:43; Ecc. 5:13; Jer. 39:10; אֵין כָּל־מְאוּמָה *not even any thing*, Gen. 39:23.
(2) *in any way* (irgend, irgendwie), *at all*. 1 Sa. 21:3, אִישׁ אַל־יֵדַע מְאוּמָה אֶת־הַדָּבָר "let no one know at all of this matter."
מָאוֹר pl. מְאֹרִים Ezek. 32:8, and מְאֹרוֹת m. Gen. 1:16 (from the root אוֹר).
(1) *light, a light*, Ps. 90:8; used of the sun and moon, Gen. 1:14, 16; Ps. 74:16. As to its difference from אוֹר see under that word. מְנוֹרַת־הַמָּאוֹר the holy candlestick, Nu. 4:9, 16. Metaph. מְאוֹר עֵינַיִם the light of the eyes, i. e. bright, cheerful eyes. Prov. 15:30.
(2) *a candlestick*, Ex. 25:6.
מְאוּרָה f. of the preceding, pr. light, hence *a hole*, through which light shines into *the den of a viper* (Lichtloch). Vulg. *caverna*. Isa. 11:8. It may also be for מְעוּרָה, מְעָרָה, غارة *cavern*, א and ע being interchanged.
מֹאזְנַיִם dual, *scales, balance*, Arab. ميزان Lev. 19:36; Job 31:6; Ps. 62:10, "that that may go up in the balance" (from lightness). Root אָזַן No. II. It differs from פֶּלֶס which see.
