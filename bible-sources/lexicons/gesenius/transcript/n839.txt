Derivatives, שָׁת, שִׁית, pr. n. שֵׁת.
שַׁיִת m. with suff. שִׁיתוֹ *a thorn*, coll. *thorns.* Isaiah 5:6; 7:23–25; 9:17; 10:17; 27:4. The etymology is doubtful. To me it seems probable that שַׁיִת is for שֶׁנֶת the Medial Nun being softened, like בַּיִת for בֶּנֶת, εἰς for ἑνς, εἰς for ἐνς, from the root שָׁנַן, whence שֵׁן fem. שֶׁנֶת, compare דֶּלֶת from דָּלַל, רֶפֶת from רָפַף. Others understand *a thorn-hedge*, as being so called from being *set* round gardens and vineyards (from שִׁית); but this word does not denote *a hedge made of thorns*, but *thorns* growing spontaneously in fields and ruins.
שִׁית m. *attire, dress*, Prov. 7:10; Psalm 73:6 Compare the root No. 2, *c*.
