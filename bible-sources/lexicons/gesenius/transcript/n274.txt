I. זָרַר an unused root, i. q. Arab. زر to bind together, as with buckles, to buckle; a kindred root to זוּר No. I, also צָרַר, צוּר. Hence the nouns זֵר, זַרְזִיר. In Chaldee there occurs זְרַז to bind, originating in the quadril. זרזר.

II. זָרַר prop. TO SCATTER; like the Arab. ذر: kindred roots זָרָה, זָרַע, זָרַק. Hence—
POEL זוֹרֵר *to sneeze*, in doing which the particles of mucus are scattered from the nostrils, 2 Ki. 4:35. Comp. Ch. זְרִיר sneezing; see Schult. ad Job. 41:10.

זֶרֶשׁ ("gold," from the Persian زر gold, with the termination ش), [*Zeresh*], pr. n. of the wife of Haman, Est. 6:13.

זֶרֶת f. *a span*, Exod. 28:16; 39:9; 1 Sam. 17:4. (Aram. ܙܪܬ[?], ܙܪܬܐ[?], זַרְתָּא id.), from the root זָרָה to spread out; whence זֵר (for זֶרֶה); f. זֶרֶת, like קֶרֶת from קָרָה, כֶּסֶת from כָּסָה.
