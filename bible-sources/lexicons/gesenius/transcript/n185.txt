Used of the carrying off of women, Jud. 21:23. In an applied sense, Job 24:19, צִיָּה גַם־חֹם יִגְזְלוּ מֵימֵי־שָׁלֶג "drought and heat take away the snow water," i. e. they absorb, drink up. It is more often used—(b) of one who *takes to himself, claims for himself* (an sich reißen) the goods of another by injustice of any kind, Job 20:19; 24:2; Mic. 2:2; especially used of the more powerful who takes for himself the goods of the weaker, whether by violence or by fraud, Levit. 5:23 ; Jer. 21:12; 22:3.
(3) with acc. of person, *to despoil* any one—(a) properly, Jud. 9:25; Ps. 35:10.—(b) by fraud and injustice, i. q. עָשַׁק. Lev. 19:13; Pro. 22:22; 28:24. Part. pass. גָּזוּל Deu. 28:29.
NIPHAL pass. *to be taken away*, used of sleep, Pro. 4:16.

II. גָּזַל an unused root, i. q. Arab. جزل *to chirp, to coo*, of a dove. Hence גּוֹזָל.
גֶּזֶל m. *rapine, robbery*, and concr. *goods obtained by force and wrong*, Lev. 5:21; Isa. 61:8; גֶּזֶל גָּזַל Eze. 22:29. Followed by a genitive, there twice occurs the form—
גֵּזֶל id. Eze. 18:18; Ecc. 5:7.
גְּזֵלָה const. גְּזֵלַת (Isa. 3:14) id. גָּזַל גְּזֵלָה Eze. 18:7, 12. גְּזֵלַת הֶעָנִי *goods taken away from the poor*, Isa. 3:14.
גָּזַם an unused root, pr. *to cut off*, like the Arabic جذم and جزم (whence جَزْم *Jesm*, the cutting off of a syllable); compare under the root גָּזַז. In the use of Hebrew language it is applied to the signification of *cutting off, devouring*, like the kindred כָּסַם, כִּרְסֵם (compare גָּזַר No. 3); whence—
גָּזָם m. *a species of locust*, so called from its eating off; like חָסִיל. Joel 1:4; 2:25; Amos 4:9; Targ. זַחֲלָא *a creeping* locust, without wings. Syriac [Syriac], which Lexicographers explain, *a locust without wings*; comp [Syriac]: *to pull in pieces*; [Syriac] *to comb*, both from the idea of plucking. LXX. κάμπη. Vulg. *eruca*. ["See Credner on Joel loc. cit."]
גַּזָּם ("eating up"), [*Gazzam*], pr. n. of a man, Ezr. 2:48; Neh. 7:51.

=== RIGHT COLUMN ===

גּוּזְנִי[?] see גּוֹזָה[?].
גָּזַע an unused root, i. q. גָּדַע No. 1, *to cut down* a tree. Compare جدع Conj. II. and جزع I. II. *to cut, to prune*; VIII. *to cut wood from a tree*. Hence—
גֶּזַע m. with suff. גִּזְעוֹ *the trunk of a felled tree*, Job 14:8; hence, *any trunk*, Isa. 11:1; and even that of a tree newly planted, and putting forth its first roots, Isa. 40:24. Arabic جذع *the trunk of a palm*; Syr. [Syriac] *a trunk, a slender stem*.
גָּזַר fut. יִגְזֹר (see No. 3), and יִגְזַר (No. 4).
(1) TO CUT, TO DIVIDE, 1 Ki. 3:25, 26; Ps. 136:13. (Arab. جزر *to cut off*; Syr. [Syriac] *to cut away, around*. Comp. the remark on גָּזַז, גָּדַד. Especially related are קָצַר, כָּזַר[?], كسر; and with the letters transposed, כָּרַת, קָרַץ, גָּרַז.)
(2) *to cut down wood*, 2 Ki. 6:4. See מַגְזֵרָה, and גַּרְזֶן *an axe*, from the kindred גָּרַז.
(3) *to eat up, to devour*, from the idea of cutting food, see בָּרָא No. 4, and בָּרָה No. 2. So fut. O. Isa. 9:19, used there of the slaughter of war. Arab. جزر *to eat quickly, to slaughter, to kill*.
(4) *to decree, to decide, to constitute*, fut. A. Job 22:28; as גְּזַר, [Syriac] in Chaldee and Syriac, compare גְּזֵרָה.
(5) intrans. *to cut off, to fail*. Hab. 3:17, גָּזַר מִמִּכְלָה צֹאן "the sheep fail in the folds." LXX. ἐξέλιπεν πρόβατα. (Arabic جزر specially used of water decreasing.)
NIPHAL—(1) pass. of Kal No. 4, *to be decreed*, Est. 2:1.
(2) *to be separated, excluded* [*cut off*]. 2 Ch. 26:21, כִּי נִגְזַר מִבֵּית יְהוָה "for he was excluded from the house of God." Isa. 53:8, כִּי נִגְזַר מֵאֶרֶץ חַיִּים "he was excluded [cut off] from the land of the living." Ps. 88:6.
(3) *to be taken away* [*cut off*], *to perish*, Lam. 3:54. With a dat. pleon. Eze. 37:11, נִגְזַרְנוּ לָנוּ "we are lost." (Arab. جزرة *calamity, destruction*.)
Derivatives follow, except מַגְזֵרָה.
גְּזַר Ch.—(1) i. q. Heb. No. 1, *to cut, to cut away*, see Ithpeal.
(2) i. q. Heb. No. 4, *to decree, to decide, to establish*, specially used of fate. Part. pl. גָּזְרִין pr. *the deciders, determiners*, hence the Chaldee astrologers, who, from the position of the stars at the hou[?]
