שָׁפַע an unused verb, *to overflow*, like the Ch., Syr. Hence —

שֶׁפַע m. *abundance*, Deu. 33:19, "abundance of the sea," i. e. wealth obtained by sea traffic.

שִׁפְעָה f. *great multitude* (pr. abundance), as of water, Job 22:11; 38:34; of men, 2 Ki. 9:17; of camels, Isa. 60:6; Eze. 26:10.

שִׁפְעִי ("abundant"), [*Shiphi*], pr. n. m. 1 Ch. 4:37.

שָׁפַף an unused root, prob. i. q. Syr. [Syriac] to glide. Hence שְׁפִיפוֹן serpent, and pr. names שְׁפוּפָם, שְׁפוּפָן, שֻׁפִּים.

שָׁפַר—(1) i. q. شفر TO SCRATCH, TO SCRAPE (cognate to סָפַר, צָפַר); hence *to polish*.
(2) *to be bright*, prop. to be polished (compare Arab. سفر I. IV., to shine forth as the dawn, and שְׁפַרְפָּרָא), *to be beautiful*, i. q. Ch. and Syr. Followed by עַל *to please* any one, Ps. 16:6, compare Dan. 4:24. The notion of being bright is also applied to brilliancy of sound ( compare, on the other hand, הָלַל); whence שׁוֹפָר trumpet.
(3) i. q. Æthiop. *safara*, to measure; whence אֶשְׁפָּר a measure; which see. (Cogn. is סָפַר No. 3, to number.) As to the passage, Job 26:13, see שִׁפְרָה.

שְׁפַר fut. יִשְׁפַּר. Chald., *to be beautiful*; followed by עַל Dan. 4:24, and קֳדָם Dan. 3:32; 6:2, *to please* (Syr. id.).
[Derivatives, שְׁפַר—שַׁפְרְפָּרָא, שׁוֹפָר, אֶשְׁפָּר.]

שֶׁפֶר m.—(1) *beauty, elegance* (of words), Gen. 49:21.
(2) [*Shapher*], pr n. of a mountain in the desert of Arabia, Num. 33:23, 24.

שִׁפְרָה f.—(1) *brightness, beauty*. Here, apparently, we should refer with Jo. Simonis, who has been followed by Ewald (Gr. page 92), Job 26:13, בְּרוּחוֹ שָׁמַיִם שִׁפְרָה " by his (God's) Spirit the heavens were made brightness," i. e. splendid, most splendid. Several interpreters have supposed שִׁפְרָה to be for שִׁפְּרָה (to make beautiful, to adorn, sc. with stars and constellations), so put that two constructions (רוּחוֹ שׁ' שִׁפְרָה[?] and בְּרוּחוֹ שָׁמַיִם שִׁפֵּר[?]) are confused (Vulg *spiritus ejus ornavit cœlos*), but Dag. forte necessarium is scarcely ever found to be omitted in the letters בגדכפת.
(2) [*Shiphrah*], pr. n. f., Ex. 1:15.

שַׁפְרִיר m., *ornaments of a throne, tapestry* with which a throne is covered, Jer. 43:10 קרי, where the כתיב has שַׁפְרוּר.

שְׁפַרְפָּרָא m., Chald., *dawn*, Dan. 6:20. Syriac [Syriac] id.

שָׁפַת fut. יִשְׁפֹּת.—(1) TO PLACE, TO PUT (i. q. שׁוּת which I suppose to be itself cognate to this verb, so that פ is softened into ב, and even into ו; compare עָפָר, עוּר; חָפַר and חוּר to dig; טָפַח and טוּחַ to spread out, and to spread over. Also שָׁפַט and שֵׁבֶט). 2 Ki. 4:38; Ezek. 24:3; Psalm 22:16, לַעֲפַר מָוֶת תִּשְׁפְּתֵנִי "into the dust of death (in the sepulchre) thou wilt put me."
(2) followed by a dat. of pers., *to give*, Isaiah 26:12.
Derivatives, מִשְׁפְּתַיִם, אַשְׁפֹּת.

שְׁפַתַּיִם m. dual, *stalls, folds*, Psa. 68:14 (compare מִשְׁפְּתַיִם p. DXX, A), Eze. 40:43 (where places in the court of the temple are signified, in which the sacrificial victims were bound).

שָׁצַף an unused verb, i. q. שָׁטַף *to inundate, to overflow*. Hence—

שֶׁצֶף, once found, Isa. 54:8, שֶׁצֶף קֶצֶף "an inundation (pouring out) of wrath," i. q. שֶׁטֶף אַף Prov. 27:4. The form שֶׁצֶף appears to have been used for שֶׁטֶף by the writer, for the sake of paronomasia.

שָׁק Chald., i. q. שׁוֹק a *leg*; pl., Dan. 2:33. Theod., κνῆμαι.
