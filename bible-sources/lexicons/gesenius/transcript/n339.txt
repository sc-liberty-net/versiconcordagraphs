(2) concr. *that which is good*, or *best* of any thing, i. e. the best part, Gen. 45:18, 20. טוּב הָאָרֶץ the best gifts of the land, Gen. 45:23; Isa. 1:19; Ezr. 9:12.

(5) *welfare, happiness*, Job 20:21; 21:16; Prov. 11:10.
(6) with the addition of לֵב *gladness, cheerfulness*. Deu. 28:47; Isa. 65:14.

טוֹבָה f.—(1) *that which is good*. לְטוֹבָה for good, Nehemiah 5:19, "remember me, O my God, לְטוֹבָה for good," i. e. that thou also wilt do good to me. Neh. 13:31. Similarly used elsewhere for accurately defining medial expressions. Ps. 86:17; Jer. 14:11; 24:6; Ezr. 8:22.
(2) *the goodness, kindness* of God, Ps. 65:12.
(3) *goods, wealth*, Ecc. 5:10.
(4) *welfare, happiness*, Ps. 16:2; 106:5.

טוֹבִיָּה & טוֹבִיָּהוּ (i. q. טוֹב לַיהוָה "pleasing to Jehovah"), pr. n. *Tobiah*, [*Tobijah*].—(1) Neh. 2:10; 4:1.—(2) Ezr. 2:60; Neh. 7:62.—(3) Zec. 6:10, 14.

טָוָה—(1) i. q. Arab. [Arabic] TO ROLL TOGETHER, TO TWIST, hence TO SPIN. Ex. 35:25, 26.
(2) *to suffer hunger, to fast*, i. q. Arab. [Arabic], whence [Arabic] hunger, [Arabic] hungry, famished. Properly, *to be twisted*, i. e. in the bowels. Thus the Arabs ascribe twisted, or entangled, bowels to those who are hungry, e. g. Hariri Cons. iii. p. 142, ed. Schult., [Arabic] to have the bowels twisted from want of food. Compare Schultens in the book just referred to, p. 4, 136.
Derivatives, טְוָת, מַטְוֶה.

טוּחַ TO SPREAD OVER, TO DAUB, as a wall with plaster, Lev. 14:42; 1 Ch. 29:4; followed by two accus. Eze. 13:10—15; 22:28; as eyes that they may not see, Isa. 44:18. (Arab. [Arabic] Med. Ye II. to cover over with fat, see Kamûs, p. 328. Compare in the western languages, τέγγω, *tingo*, tünchen.) Isaiah loc. cit. the pret. is טַח for טָח (as if from טָחַח).
NIPHAL pass. Lev. 14:43, 48.
Derivatives, טִיחַ, טֻחוֹת[?].

=== RIGHT COLUMN ===
טוּט an unused root, see טִיט. ["Arab. [Arabic] to collect." To this root in Thes. are referred טִיט, טָאטָא and מַטְאֲטֵא.]

טוֹטָפוֹת pl. f. *bands, fillets*, especially those worn by the Jews at prayers (תְּפִלִּין[?], φυλακτήρια, Matt. 23:5), i. e. scrolls of parchment with sentences written on them out of the law of Moses (Ex. 13:1—10, 11—16; Deu. 6:4—9; 11:13—21), which the Jews have been accustomed to wear at prayers bound to the forehead and the left wrist, Ex. 13:16; Deu. 6:8; 11:18. [It requires proof that the Jewish phylacteries are here intended by these fillets or bandages.] (Ch. טוֹטַפְתָּא, טוֹטְפָא a bracelet, a frontlet. This word is for טַפְטָפָה[?], like בָּבֶל for בַּלְבֵּל, כּוֹכָב for כִּבְכָּב, גֻּלְגֹּלֶת, Syr. [Syriac], Lehrg. p. 869 Root טוּף, which see; and not טָפַף, to which the signification of binding has been hastily attributed.)

טוּל unused in Kal. Arab. [Arabic] Med. Waw, TO BE LONG.
HIPHIL הֵטִיל *to throw down at length, to prostrate* (der Länge lang hinwerfen, comp. Isai. 22:17); *to throw, to cast*, as a spear, 1 Sa. 18:11; 20:33; *to cast out* as from a country, Jer. 16:13; 22:26; from a ship, Jon. 1:5, 12; *to send forth* a wind, Jon. 1:4.
HOPHAL, *to be prostrated*, Ps. 37:24; Job 41:1; *to be cast* as a lot, Prov. 16:33; *to be cast out*, Jer. 22:28.
PILPEL, טִלְטֵל i. q. Hiphil, *to prostrate, to cast forth*, Isa. 22:17.
Derivative, טַלְטֵלָה.

טוּף an unused root. Arab. [Arabic] Med. Waw, *to surround*.
Hence טוֹטָפוֹת bands, as if girdles.

טוּר an unused root, like the kindred roots דּוּר, תּוּר *to surround* (see Hartmann's Linguist. Einleit. p. 82). Hence טוּר [טִירָה, יְטוּר[?]].

טוּר m.—(1) *a wall* around about, *a fence, an inclosure*. Eze. 46:23. ([Arabic] a boundary, [Arabic] a fence.)
(2) *a row*, as of precious stones. Ex. 28:17, seq.; 39:10, seq. ["Or of hewn stone, 1 Ki. 7:12; also applied to *a row* of other things."]

טוּר Ch. m. *a mountain, a rock*. Dan. 2:35, 45; i. q. Heb. צוּר. Syr. [Syriac] id. ["Arab. [Arabic]."]
