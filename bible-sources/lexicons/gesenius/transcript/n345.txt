יָאַב TO DESIRE, TO LONG, followed by לְ. Ps. 119:131. ["LXX. ἐπιπόθουν."] (Syr. [Syriac] and the quadriliteral [Syriac] id. Kindred roots are אָבָה, אָוָה).

יָאָה TO BE SEEMLY, BECOMING, i. q. נָאָה, נָאוָה (Ps. 33:1; Prov. 17:7). Impers. followed by לְ *to become* some one. Jer. 10:7, כִּי לְךָ יָאָתָה "for it becometh thee." LXX. ed. Compl. σοὶ γὰρ πρέπει. (Syr. [Syriac], suitable, seemly, followed by [Syriac] becoming, πρέπον.)

יָאִיר ("whom he (sc. God) enlightens" from אוּר), [*Jair*], pr. n. (Gr. Ἰάειρος, Mark 5:22)—(1) of a son of Manasseh, Nu. 32:41.—(2) of a judge of the Israelites, Jud. 10:3.—(3) Est. 2:5. Patron. יָאִרִי 2 Sa. 20:26; from No. 2.

יְאֹר & יְאוֹר masc. A RIVER, an Egyptian word, in the Memphitic dialect IAPO, in the Sahidic IEPO (see Jablonskii Opuscc. ed. te Water, tom. i. page 93, 444; Champollion, l'Egypte, i. p. 137, 138; ii. 238); on the Rosetta stone ["as read by Dr. Young"] is found, lines 14, 15, IOR (see Kosegarten, De Scriptura Vett. Ægyptiorum, p. 14). It is used almost exclusively of *the Nile*. Gen. 41:1, seq.; Exod. 1:22; 2:3; 7:15, seq.; in one passage, of another river Dan. 12:5, 6, 7.
Plural יְאֹרִים *rivers, channels*, Job 28:10; Isa. 33:21; specially the arms and channels of the Nile, Eze. 29:3, seq.; 30:12; Ps. 78:44; hence יְאֹרֵי מִצְרַיִם, יְאֹרֵי מָצוֹר Isa. 7:18; 19:6; 37:25.

יָאַשׁ unused in Kal. Arabic [Arabic] and transp. [Arabic] *to despair, to be cast down in spirit*.
NIPHAL id., followed by מִן *to desist from any person* or *thing*. 1 Sa. 27:1, וְנוֹאַשׁ מִמֶּנִּי שָׁאוּל לְבַקְשֵׁנִי עוֹד "Saul will desist from me to seek me any more." Part. נוֹאָשׁ *void of hope*, Job 6:26. Neutr. *to be without hope, to be in vain*, Isa. 57:10; Jer. 2:25; 18:12.
PIEL inf. יָאֵשׁ[?], followed by לִבּוֹ[?] *to give over to despair* Ecc. 2:20.

יֹאשִׁיָּה (see the following word), [*Josiah*], pr. n. m., Zec. 6:10.

יֹאשִׁיָּהוּ pr. n. ("whom Jehovah heals," from the root אָשָׁה = [Arabic] to heal, and יְהוּ), *Josiah*, king of Judah, 642—611 B.C., restorer of the observance of the law of Moses: slain in battle at Megiddo, by Necho, king of Egypt, 2 Ki. 23:23; 2 Chr. 34:33. Greek Ἰωσίας.

יְאָתְרַי [*Jeaterai*] pr. n. m., 1 Ch. 6:6; for which there is in verse 26, אֶתְנִי.
