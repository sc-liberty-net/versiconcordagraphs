RUNNING HEADS: שָׁאָה–שְׁאֵרָה[?] | DCCXCVIII | שָׁאָה–שָׁאַל
שָׁאָה see שׁוֹאָה.
שָׁאֲוָה Pro.1:27; כתיב i.q. שׁוֹאָה a wasting tempest.
שְׁאוֹל, שְׁאֹל, com. (m. Job 26:6; f. Isa. 5:14; 14:9); *orcus, hades*, a subterranean place, full of thick darkness (Job 10:21, 22), in which the shades of the dead are gathered together (רְפָאִים which see), and to which are attributed both valleys [rather depths] (Pro. 9:18) and gates (Isa. 38:10); Gen. 37:35; Num. 16:30, seqq., Psa. 6:6; Isa. 14:9, seqq., 38:18; Eze. 31:16, seqq., 32:21, seqq. (Syr. [Syriac] f. and Æth. [Ethiopic]: hell, purgatory, *limbus Patrum*. I think that I have lighted on the true etymology of the word. For I have no doubt that שְׁאוֹל is for שָׁעוֹל *a hollow, a hollow and subterranean place*, just as the Germ. Hölle is of the same origin as Höhle, and Lat. *cælum* is from the Gr. κοῖλος, hohl, hollow. It is commonly derived from the idea of asking, from its asking for, demanding all, without distinction; hence *orcus rapax*, Catull. ii. 28, 29.)
שָׁאוּל ("asked for," compare 1 Sam. 8:4, seq.) *Saul*, [*Shaul*], pr. n.—(1) of the first Israelite king, of the tribe of Benjamin, 1 Sa. 9:2, seq.—(2) of a king of the Edomites, Gen. 36:37.—(3) of a son of Simeon, 46:10.—(4) 1 Ch. 6:9. From 3 is patron. שָׁאוּלִי [*Shaulites*], Nu. 26:13.
שָׁאוֹן m. (from the root שָׁאָה—(1) *noise, tumult*, as of waters, Ps. 65:8; of a great crowd of men, Isa. 5:14; 13:4; 24:8; of war, Am. 2:2; Hos. 10:14; of clamour, Ps. 74:23; Jer. 25:31. Jer. 48:45, בְּנֵי שָׁאוֹן " the sons of noise," i. e. tumultuous soldiers.
(2) *devastation, destruction*. Psalm 40:3, בּוֹר שָׁאוֹן " the pit of destruction;" Jer. 46:17.
[" שָׁאָט an unused root, i. q. שׁוּט No. II, *to loathe*, Hence—"]
שָׁאט m. (with Kametz impure) with suff. שָׁאטְךָ Ezek. 25:6, *contempt*, hence *pride, arrogance*, 36:5; compare 25:15. Root שׁוּט [שָׁאַט in Thes.].
שְׁאִיָּה f. *ruins*, Isa. 24:12; from the root שָׁאָה.
I. שָׁאַל & שָׁאֵל fut. יִשְׁאַל (Syr. [Syriac], Arab. سال).
(1) TO ASK, TO DEMAND, followed by an acc. of the thing, 1 Sa. 12:13; and מִן Ps. 2:8; מֵאֵת 1 Sa. 8:10, of the person from whom any thing is asked, also followed by two acc. (αἰτεῖν τινά τι, to ask any one any thing), Psa. 137:3; Deu. 14:26; Isaiah 45:11. Job 31:30, לִשְׁאֹל בְּאָלָה נַפְשׁוֹ " by asking with a curse his (an enemy's) life," i. e. for his death, comp.

=== RIGHT COLUMN ===
Jon. 4:8, וַיִּשְׁאַל אֶת־נַפְשׁוֹ לָמוּת "and he desired death for himself;" 1 Ki. 19:4.
(2) *to ask, to request* any thing from any one, Isaiah 7:11 (see עָמַק Hiphil), שָׁאַל שְׁאֵלָה see שְׁאֵלָה. Followed by an acc. of thing, Jud. 5:25; 1 Kings 3:10; מִן of the person from whom it is asked, Psa. 21:5; also מֵאֵת 1 Ki. 2:20; מֵעִם[?] Deu. 18:16; שָׁאַל דָּבָר לְ to ask any thing for any one, 1 Ki. 2 : 22.
Specially—(*a*) *to ask a loan, to borrow* from any one, Ex. 3:22; 11:2; 12:35. Part. שָׁאוּל lent, 1 Sa. 1:28; 2 Ki. 6:5. Followed by לְ prob. *to lend*, i. q. Hiphil, 1 Sa. 2:20.—(*b*) *to ask alms, to beg*, i. q. Piel No. 1, Prov. 20:4. (Arab. Conj. V. id., سايل a beggar.)
(3) *to inquire of, to interrogate*, Jud. 4:20, seq.; followed by an acc. of pers., Gen. 24:47; Job 40:7; Josh. 9:14, "but they inquired not at the mouth of Jehovah" (which they ought to have done); compare Isa. 30:2; Gen. 24:57; followed by לְ of pers., Job 8:8. The person or thing asked *about*, has לְ before it, Jud. 13:18; Gen. 32:30; עַל Neh. 1:2; acc., Hag. 2:11; Isa. 45:11. Specially it is—(*a*) *to consult*; followed by בְּ (sich befragen bey), as שָׁאַל בַּיהוָֹה to consult the oracle of Jehovah, Jud. 1:1; 18:5; 20:18; שָׁאַל בַּתְּרָפִים to consult Teraphim, Eze. 21:26; Followed by לְ for any one, for his benefit, 1 Sam. 22:10, 13, 15; Num. 27:21.—(*b*) שָׁאַל לְפ׳ לְשָׁלוֹם[?] to ask of any one's health; hence *to salute*, Gen. 43:27; 1 Sam. 10:4; 17:22; 30:21; Ex. 18:7.
NIPHAL, *to ask for oneself, to ask leave* (like the Gr. αἰτοῦμαί σε τοῦτο; Germ. sich etwas ausbitten; followed by מִן of pers. and a gerund of the thing, to do which one asks for leave, 1 Sa. 20:6, 28 (where the verb is omitted); followed by a finite verb, Neh. 13:6. Comp. Gram. § 50, 2. Others take it, to obtain by prayers, leave or liberty from a master; Germ. sich frey=losbitten.
PIEL שִׁאֵל—(1) *to beg, to be a beggar*, i. q. Kal No. 2, *b*; Ps. 109:10.
(2) *to question*, 2 Sam. 20:18.
HIPHIL, *to lend*, Ex. 12:36; 1 Sam. 1:28; compare Kal No. 2, *a*.
Derivatives, שְׁאֵלָה, מִשְׁאָלָה; and the pr. n. שְׁאָל, שָׁאוּל, שֵׁלָה, שְׁאַלְתִּיאֵל, אֶשְׁתָּאוֹל.
II. שָׁאַל i. q. שָׁעַל *to be hollow*; hence שְׁאוֹל hades, as if a subterranean cave. [In Thes. I. and II. are joined; *to dig, to excavate*, is taken as the primary meaning.]
שְׁאָל ("prayer"), [*Sheal*], pr. n. m. Ezr 10:29.
שְׁאֵל Ch.—(1) *to ask, to demand*, Dan. 2:10, 11; followed by two acc. Ezr. 7:21.
