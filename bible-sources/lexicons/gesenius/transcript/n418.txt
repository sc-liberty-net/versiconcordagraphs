RUNNING HEADS: כלה־כליא | CCCXCIX | כלא־כליא
PUAL כֻּלָּה [?] and כָּלָּה [?] *to be completed, finished*. Gen. 2:1; Ps. 72:20.
Derivatives, כַּלָּה, כָּלָה, כְּלִי, כִּלְיָה, כִּלָּיוֹן, תַּכְלִית, תִּכְלָה, מִכְלוֹת, and the proper names כְּלוּהַי, כִּלְיוֹן.
כָּלֶה adj. f. כָּלָה *pining away* (used of the eye); compare the verb No. 3. Deut. 28:32.
כָּלָה fem.—(1) *completion, perfection*; hence adv. לְכָלָה 2 Ch. 12:12; Eze. 13:13, and כָּלָה *altogether*, Gen. 18:21; Ex. 11:1.
(2) *consumption, destruction*, עָשָׂה כָלָה to make consumption, to destroy altogether; Jer. 4:27; 5:10; Neh. 9:31; Nah. 1:8, 9. Followed by בְּ, Jer. 30:11, and אֵת of pers. Jer. 5:18; 46:28; Eze. 11:13; 20:17.
כַּלָּה fem.—(1) *a bride, maiden betrothed*, so called from her being crowned with a chaplet, see כָּלַל No. 2. Cant. 4:8, seq.; Jer. 2:32; 7:34; 16:9; 25:10. [Syr. ܟܠܬܐ pl. [Syriac] id.]
(2) *daughter-in-law*, Gen. 38:11, 24; Lev. 18:15; Ruth 4:15. Compare חָתָן.
כְּלוּא m. *prison*, i. q. כֶּלֶא Jer. 37:4; 52:31 קרי. In כתיב there is כְּלִיא.
כְּלוּב m. *wicker-work*, woven of twigs or rods, from the root כָּלַב No. II., specially—(1) *a basket* for fruit, Am. 8:1.
(2) *a cage* for birds, Jer. 5:27. (Syr. ܟܠܘܒܐ id., and the same word is also adopted in Greek, κλωβός, κλουβός, κλοβός, *a cage*, see Bochart, Hieroz. i. 662, ii. p. 90). It is also pr. n. m. [*Chelub*].—(*a*) 1 Ch. 4:11.—(*b*) 1 Ch. 27:26.
כְּלוּבַי see כָּלֵב No. 2.
כְּלוּהַי, כְּלוּהוּ קרי [*Chelluh*], pr. n. m. Ezr. 10:35.
כְּלוּלוֹת f. pl. denom. from כַּלָּה; *the state*, or *condition of a bride* before her marriage, Brautstand, Jer. 2:2.
כָּלַח an unused root, to which interpreters have assigned various meanings. However I have no doubt but that it signifies the same as כָּלָה *to be completed, finished* (compare קָשָׁה and קָשַׁח and the examples given below on the root קָלַח [?]). Hence—
כֶּלַח m.—(1) *completion, finishing* [this meaning is not given in Thes. see No. 2]. So Job 30:2, used of very despicable men; " what can the strength of their hands profit me עָלֵימוֹ אָבַד כָּלַח in whom completion is perished," who cannot complete any thing. LXX. ἐπ' αὐτοὺς ἀπώλετο συντέλεια. עָלֵימוֹ for (אֲשֶׁר) לָמוֹ. Hence—

(2) poetically used of *old age*, as rightly taken by Targ. Saad. Ms. Kimchi: (the Arab. كلم to have an austere countenance, and كلح to draw up and contract the lips, are secondary words, both of them being derived from the idea of old age). Job 5:26, תָּבוֹא בְכֶלַח אֱלֵי קָבֶר " thou shalt go to the grave in old age," as if בְּשֵׂיבָה טוֹבָה. As to the word with which I, together with others, formerly compared this, " Syr. [Syriac] soundness, health;" it rested on a singular error of Edm. Castell, who had incorrectly rendered a gloss of Barbahlul, see Lex. min. edit. 3, pref. p. xx. [In Thes. the primary meaning given to this word is " perhaps *mature old age*."]
(3) [*Calah*], pr. n. of a city and province of Assyria, probably the same as is elsewhere written חֲלַח, which see. (Compare כְּבָר and חָבוֹר.) Gen. 10:11. See Michaëlis, Supplem. p. 767.
כְּלִי m. in pause כֶּלִי pl. כֵּלִים (from the lost sing. כָּלֶה) const. כְּלִי properly whatever is made, completed, or prepared, from the root כָּלָה, a word of very general import, like the Germ. Zeug from zeugen, i. e. to complete = τεύχειν, specially—
(1) any *utensil, vessel*. Gen. 31:37; 45:20. כְּלֵי זָהָב, כְּלֵי כֶסֶף vessels of gold, of silver (Silberzeug). Ex. 3:22; 11:2. כְּלֵי בֵית־יְהוָה Ezr. 1:7, and כְּלֵי יְהוָה Isa. 52:11, the vessels of the temple. כְּלֵי גוֹלָה vessels of wandering, outfit for exile (Wanderzeug), Jer. 46:19.
(2) *clothing* (Zeug), ornaments. כְּלִי גֶבֶר a man's clothing, Deut. 22:5; used of the ornaments of a bride, Isa. 61:10; also of yokes for oxen, 2 Sa. 24:22.
(3) *a vessel* for sailing (Fahrzeug). Isa. 18:2.
(4) *an implement, a tool* (Werkzeug). כְּלֵי שִׁיר musical instruments, 2 Chr. 34:12; Am. 6:5. כְּלִי־נֶבֶל pleon. instrument of a psaltery, Psalm 71:22. Metaph. כְּלֵי זַעַם יְהוָה instruments of the indignation of Jehovah. Isai. 13:5; Jer. 50:25. Isai. 32:7, כֵּלַי כֵּלָיו רָעִים " (as to) the deceiver his instruments are evil," i. e. the devices which he uses to carry out his plans. Gen. 49:5.
(5) *arms, weapons* (Rüstzeug), Gen. 27:3; more fully, [כְּלֵי] מִלְחָמָה Jud. 18:11, 16. כְּלֵי־מָוֶת deadly weapons, Psalm 7:14. נֹשֵׂא כֵלִים an armour-bearer, 1 Sa. 14:1, 6, 7, seq.; 31:4, 5, 6. בֵּית כֵּלִים an armoury (Zeughaus). Isa. 39:2.
כֵּלַי [?] see כִּילַי.
כְּלִיא m. *a prison*. Jer. 37:4; 52:31, כתיב. Root כָּלָא.
