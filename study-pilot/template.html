Word divisions, lemmas, grammar tags, Bible-wide counts and the brief lexicons (including Abbott-Smith's Greek lexicon) are from STEP Bible, Tyndale House Cambridge, CC BY 4.0. BDB entries are from unfoldingWord's Brown-Driver-Briggs Enhanced, CC BY. Gesenius (Tregelles, 1857) is read from this project's own transcripts of the archive.org scan; Thayer (1889) is machine-read from its scan. Both are public domain. The Septuagint is Swete's edition (1909-1930) as digitised by the Open Greek and Latin Project's First1KGreek, prepared by nathans/lxx-swete, CC BY-SA 4.0; its dictionary forms and Strong's numbers are from the Open Scriptures Septuagint Project, CC BY 4.0, matched to Swete's words by this project.

All other translations shown are public domain. Hebrew is shown with vowel points but without cantillation marks. The verse summaries are this project's own paraphrase. The English rendering and everything under "In this verse" are drafts written for this pilot and have not been reviewed.
